# QXAMP

**Learn by quizzing. Remember by repeating.**

QXAMP transforms notes, articles, and study materials into interactive quizzes—instantly. Paste in your content, choose how many questions you want, and let AI help you create a quiz you can revisit anytime, anywhere.

Anonymous, community-built, and free to explore, QXAMP makes it easier to turn studying into a habit that sticks.

## Get started

1. Paste your notes, an article, or other study material.
2. Choose how many questions you’d like.
3. Generate a quiz and revisit it whenever you’re ready to study.
